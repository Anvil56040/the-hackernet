What is this place?

The internet has a problem. Where has its personality gone? It's all corporate and AI now, and the whole reason the internet was created has been thrown in the bin. People are illusioned into thinking they are happy by big monopolies like meta and google, so we need a sanctuary. This site, the Hackernet, is my answer to this. I created it originally because of my new domain I bought, but I'm evolving it into what I hope will bring at least a small portion of the internet together. Theres a chat room, search engine (go to the second page for more info) and I hope to add more.
